# Draft Modif encodings

All SRD 5.2.1 character content is now in the pack with its Effects: every class, subclass, species, background and feat, all spells and magic items, and the equipment, tools, gear, conditions, rules, hazards and poisons. The pack files are the source of truth.

What's left here:

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `weapon-armor-rows.yaml` | The weapon and armor table rows as structured data (made by a converter). Reference only: the app reads these tables from `rules/equipment/` in the pack. |

Monsters aren't encoded yet (planned for a later Modif version). New drafts for them, or for homebrew packs, can go here while they're being written; follow [`docs/CONVERTING_TO_MODIF.md`](../docs/CONVERTING_TO_MODIF.md) and delete a draft once its entry is in the pack.
