## Known source quirks

- *Phantasmal Force* appears in the spell descriptions (Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard) but not on any class spell list in the SRD. Both are kept as printed.
- "A Glass Bottle holds up to 11/2 pints" — the source PDF prints "11/2" (a lost ½). Kept as printed.
